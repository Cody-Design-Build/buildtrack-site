Agent first

Every part of BuildTrack is a plugin.

Jobs, budgets, bids, invoices, bills, the schedule, job costing: each one is a small folder of code that describes itself to the app. Don't like how job costing works? Rewrite the plugin, not the software.

  • Switch off what you don't use. Settings → Plugins turns features on and off for the whole company. The records stay, so turning one back on brings it back as it was.
  • Replace, don't fork. Give your plugin the same role as the one it replaces and pick it in Settings. Everything else keeps working.
  • Contracts, not tangles. Plugins never reach into each other's code. They share data through versioned contracts: budgets, bids and bills hand their numbers to job costing as costs/v1, so rewriting one never breaks another.
  • Written for agents. One folder, one definePlugin(), and a guide that walks your agent through it. Describe the feature; your agent builds the plugin.

MCP built in

Your AI agent can run BuildTrack.

Plugins let your agent change BuildTrack. Its own MCP server, on the new MCP spec (what people are calling MCP 2.0), lets your agent use it: look up a job, write a proposal, bill a job, pay a bill, put work out to bid, all as you.

  • Connect in a minute. Add your BuildTrack address plus /mcp as a connector in Claude, or claude mcp add in Claude Code. You sign in as usual and approve the agent; it acts as you, with your access and nothing more.
  • Does the work, not just reports. Proposals, change orders, progress invoices, bills and payments, bid packages and awards, tasks, settings and the team, through the same rules as the app.
  • Asks before anything that matters. Sending, approving, payments, awards and team changes wait for your yes, in a form right in the chat.
  • Skills your company owns. Each plugin ships skills that teach agents how it works. Edit them, turn them off or add your own in Settings → Agents, or just ask the agent to.
  • Follows your plugins. Turn a plugin off and its tools and skills are gone for every agent; turn one on and it's there on the next request. Nothing to reinstall.

Example session

Make it yours

Your company's look, not ours.

Start from 13 themes in three styles: clean and businesslike, soft and rounded, or terminal. Each brings its own colours, font and corners, and you can change any of them. Set one look for the whole company, or let everyone pick their own.

Features

Everything a job needs, from first estimate to final invoice.

Proposals and change orders your clients can read

Every proposal and change order is its own budget with its own customer document. Show every line, group totals or one price; send it, lock it, record the signature. Change orders carry the running approved total, so nobody argues about the contract.

Progress invoicing against what was approved

Bill by percent complete, line by line or by group. BuildTrack works out this invoice, billed to date and balance to finish, then tracks payments and what's overdue.

Bids, awards and vendor bills

Put scopes out to bid, compare subcontractor numbers side by side, award the winner, then record their bills and payments. Every cost lands on a cost code, so job costing is always current.

A schedule the whole crew can see

A Gantt chart across every job, with crew assignments and progress, plus a plain list when that's all you need. Export to your calendar.

Jobs board

Leads to closeout on one board, with the customer, address and contract total on every card.

Job costing

Budget, committed and actual cost by cost code for every job, updated as bids are awarded and bills come in.

People and accounts

Households, companies and vendors, with the people in them and their role on each job.

The whole team, live

Everyone works on the same data; changes show up on teammates' screens as they're saved.

Invite-only

Nobody gets an account without an invite. Owners and admins invite the team; people sign in with an emailed code.

Shared files

Signed proposals and bill attachments are stored once and open for the whole team.

Tasks

Follow-ups and to-dos by job, type and who's on it.

Undo and backups

Undo a change you didn't mean, and download a full backup of your data whenever you like.

Your look

Light or dark, 13 themes to start from, and any colour, font or corner you like.

Built for the agentic era

Stop filing feature requests. Ask your agent.

BuildTrack is written to be changed by AI coding agents like Claude Code or Codex. Describe what your company needs in plain words; your agent makes the change, and you check it before anyone else sees it.

  • Agents know the rules. AGENTS.md and the plugin guide walk any agent through the codebase, the data and the release process.
  • A data model you can explain in a sentence. Every record is one row; a new kind of record needs no database migration.
  • Every change gets a preview. Each pull request builds its own private link with sample data, so you see the change before your team does.
  • Small enough to understand. React and TypeScript, no framework maze. You, or your agent, can read the whole thing.

Own it

Run it your way.

1

Try it in a minute

Open the demo, or run it on your machine with sample jobs, budgets and invoices. No accounts, no setup.

git clone https://github.com/Cody-Design-Build/BuildTrack && cd BuildTrack && npm install && npm run dev
2

Put your team on it

Connect your own Supabase database and deploy to Vercel. Invite your team; they sign in with an emailed code and share live data.

3

Make it yours

Change anything. It's open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence: use it, adapt it, and if you offer your changed version to others, share the changes back.
